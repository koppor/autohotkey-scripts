## 2026-05-04

### Changed

- Consolidated all scripts into a single [MyScripts.ahk](MyScripts.ahk) with a shared `ActivateOrRun` helper. Toggle a binding by commenting or uncommenting its line.

### Removed

- Per-application `.ahk` files (`Firefox.ahk`, `Chrome.ahk`, `IntelliJ.ahk`, etc.) — superseded by `MyScripts.ahk`.
